## How Datacendia Helps Arsenal

### Scenario 1: Player Transfer Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Arsenal's Problem:** Arsenal have invested £600M+ in transfers since 2021 to build a title-challenging squad. Every AI-scouted recommendation must be defensible to KSE's board (who also govern a $10B+ multi-sport empire) and Premier League PSR.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every transfer input and outcome. Cryptographically signed by sporting director and CFO. Tamper-proof receipt for KSE governance, Premier League, and UEFA.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR, KSE corporate governance

### Scenario 2: StatDNA Integration — Arsenal's Own AI Platform
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision` + `CendiaPrecedent`
**Arsenal's Problem:** Arsenal acquired StatDNA (AI scouting analytics) in 2012 — one of the first clubs to buy an analytics company. StatDNA's AI models drive recruitment recommendations, but the models themselves have no governance layer. If StatDNA recommends a £60M target who underperforms, can Arsenal prove the AI's reasoning was sound?
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway wraps StatDNA's AI outputs with evidence capture. Every recommendation gets a decision receipt. CendiaPrecedent compares new recommendations against historical StatDNA outcomes to flag inconsistencies.
**Applicable Regulations:** EU AI Act (high-risk employment decisions), Premier League PSR

### Scenario 3: KSE Multi-Sport Governance
**Arsenal's Problem:** KSE own Arsenal, LA Rams, Denver Nuggets, Colorado Avalanche, Colorado Rapids, and ENOS (e-sports). AI governance standards set at Arsenal could cascade across the entire KSE portfolio. KSE board needs consistent AI decision evidence across all properties.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Datacendia's vertical-agnostic architecture means the same platform deployed at Arsenal can extend to the Rams (NFL), Nuggets (NBA), Avalanche (NHL). One governance standard, multiple sports.
**Applicable Regulations:** Cross-sport regulatory frameworks

### Scenario 4: Premier League PSR Compliance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Arsenal's Problem:** After heavy investment, Arsenal must balance title ambition with PSR limits (£105M losses over 3 years). AI financial modelling for amortization, wage projections, and commercial revenue forecasting must be auditable.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Auto-flags when spending approaches PSR thresholds. Tracks wage-to-revenue ratio, related party transactions, break-even. Evidence trail for Premier League compliance.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 5: Emirates Stadium Operations
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`

**Applicable Regulations:** Safety of Sports Grounds Act, local authority licensing
