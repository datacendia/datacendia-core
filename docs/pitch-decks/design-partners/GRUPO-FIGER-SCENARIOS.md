## SECTION A: Player Representation & Transfer Governance (Scenarios 1–50)

### Scenario 1: Player Transfer Mandate Governance
**Decision Type:** Transfer Decision
**Grupo Figer's Problem:** Figer represents 200+ players. Each transfer mandate must be documented under FIFA Agent Regulations 2023 — written mandate, fee agreement, duration, scope of representation. Failure to document correctly voids the agent's fee entitlement.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every mandate captured: player identity, mandate scope, duration, fee percentage, FIFA registration reference, player acknowledgment. Cryptographically signed and timestamped. Evidence for FIFA dispute resolution if fee is contested.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIFA Agent Regulations 2023 Article 14, FIFA RSTP

### Scenario 2: Agent Fee Cap Compliance
**Decision Type:** Transfer Decision
**Grupo Figer's Problem:** FIFA Agent Regulations 2023 introduced fee caps — 3% of player remuneration for player-side agent, 3% for club-side agent, 6% for dual representation. Figer must document every fee calculation to avoid FIFA sanctions.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every fee captured: gross player remuneration, applicable cap percentage, calculated maximum, agreed fee, compliance status. Hard-stop if fee exceeds FIFA cap. Evidence for FIFA examination.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIFA Agent Regulations 2023 Articles 15-16

### Scenario 3: Dual Representation Governance
**Decision Type:** Transfer Decision
**Grupo Figer's Problem:** FIFA permits dual representation (acting for both player and club in the same transfer) only with explicit written consent from both parties. Figer frequently acts in dual capacity. Without documented consent, the entire fee is at risk.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures dual representation: player consent, club consent, conflict disclosure, fee allocation between mandates, combined fee cap compliance. Hard-stop without both consents. Evidence for FIFA dispute.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIFA Agent Regulations 2023 Articles 12-13
