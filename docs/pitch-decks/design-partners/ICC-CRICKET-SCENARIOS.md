## How Datacendia Helps the ICC

### Scenario 1: Decision Review System (DRS) Evidence
**Decision Type:** Custom — Officiating Decision
**ICC's Problem:** DRS uses AI (ball-tracking by Hawk-Eye, Snickometer audio analysis, UltraEdge edge detection, Hot Spot thermal imaging) to review umpire decisions. DRS overturns affect match outcomes worth $100M+ in broadcast revenue. The "umpire's call" zone is cricket's most contentious AI issue — when ball-tracking shows the ball clipping the stumps, the on-field decision stands. Teams and fans demand transparency on AI accuracy, calibration, and margin of error.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every DRS decision captured: ball-tracking data, prediction confidence, Hawk-Eye calibration status, Snickometer audio, UltraEdge visual, human umpire decision, final outcome. Cryptographically hashed. Teams can request a Regulator's Receipt for any review. Evidence for ICC Match Referee reports and appeals.
**Applicable Regulations:** ICC Playing Conditions, DRS Protocol, CAS

### Scenario 2: IPL Governance ($10B+ Ecosystem)
**ICC's Problem:** The Indian Premier League is cricket's commercial powerhouse — franchises worth $5B+ (Mumbai Indians), annual broadcast rights $6.2B (2023-2027). IPL uses AI for auction strategy, player analytics, and performance prediction. The BCCI (Board of Control for Cricket in India) governs IPL with ICC oversight. AI-driven auction recommendations, retention decisions, and salary cap compliance need governance.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for IPL franchise AI operations — auction strategy rationale, retention analysis, salary cap compliance. Evidence for BCCI, ICC, and franchise investor governance.
**Applicable Regulations:** IPL Regulations, BCCI governance, ICC oversight, Indian corporate law

### Scenario 3: Match-Fixing — Anti-Corruption Unit
**Decision Type:** `MatchIntegrityDecision`
**ICC's Problem:** Cricket has the most extensive match-fixing history in major sport — Hansie Cronje (2000), Pakistan spot-fixing (2010), multiple IPL fixing scandals. The ICC Anti-Corruption Unit (ACU) uses AI for betting pattern analysis, player communication monitoring, and corruptor network mapping. Investigation evidence must satisfy national criminal courts (not just CAS).
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures ACU investigation process: AI-detected anomalies, player interviews, evidence collection, corruptor links. Court-admissible evidence chain for national prosecutors (UK Crown Prosecution, Indian CBI, Pakistan FIA).
**Applicable Regulations:** ICC Anti-Corruption Code, national criminal law, Interpol

### Scenario 4: Bio-Secure Bubble / Player Welfare AI
**ICC's Problem:** Post-COVID, cricket used AI for bio-secure bubble management, mental health monitoring, and workload management. Player welfare AI (tracking fatigue, mental health indicators, injury risk) involves sensitive personal data across 108 nations with different data protection laws.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Player welfare data protection with cross-jurisdiction compliance. Evidence of appropriate AI use for mental health monitoring (critical given duty of care obligations).
**Applicable Regulations:** GDPR (UK/EU), India DPDPA, Australia Privacy Act, 100+ national data laws

### Scenario 5: Revenue Distribution to 108 Nations
**ICC's Problem:** ICC distributes billions to 108 member nations. The BCCI (India) receives the largest share due to commercial contribution. AI-driven allocation models must be transparent — smaller nations challenge distribution fairness.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for every allocation. CendiaPrecedent tracks consistency and fairness across nations.
**Applicable Regulations:** ICC Constitution, member agreements
