### Scenario 1: Post-Corruption Governance Evidence
**Decision Type:** All decision types
**Benfica's Problem:** Benfica have been implicated in Portuguese football corruption investigations — Operation E-toupeira (alleged match-fixing information), Operation Lex (alleged judicial corruption). Former president Luís Filipe Vieira was arrested. Every AI-assisted decision must now demonstrate integrity under enhanced scrutiny from Portuguese prosecutors, CMVM (securities regulator), and UEFA.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every decision produces a Regulator's Receipt with cryptographic proof. Evidence of reformed governance — not just policies, but verifiable decision integrity. Exportable for Portuguese prosecutors, CMVM, and UEFA.
**Applicable Regulations:** Portuguese criminal law, CMVM, Liga Portugal, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 2: World-Record 260,000 Socios Transparency
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
