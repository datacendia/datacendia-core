## How Datacendia Helps Liverpool

### Scenario 1: Data-Driven Transfer Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Liverpool's Problem:** Liverpool pioneered the "Moneyball" approach to football — Ian Graham's research department built proprietary AI models that identified targets like Salah, Robertson, and Alexander-Arnold's development path. Under new sporting director Richard Hughes, these AI models continue to drive £100M+ transfer windows. FSG's board (who applied the same approach at the Red Sox) demands audit-grade evidence of AI recommendations.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every transfer input, AI model recommendation, and outcome. Cryptographically signed by sporting director and CFO. Evidence for FSG governance, Premier League PSR, and UEFA FSR.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR, FSG corporate governance

### Scenario 2: FSG Multi-Sport AI Governance
**Liverpool's Problem:** FSG own Liverpool, Boston Red Sox, Pittsburgh Penguins, and have stakes in other sports properties. AI governance standards established at Liverpool cascade across FSG's portfolio — each sport has different regulatory requirements.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Vertical-agnostic architecture means the same Datacendia platform at Liverpool extends to MLB (Red Sox), NHL (Penguins). One evidence standard, multiple sports.
**Applicable Regulations:** Cross-sport regulatory frameworks, US/UK/European jurisdictions

### Scenario 3: Premier League PSR Compliance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Liverpool's Problem:** Liverpool balance competitive spending with PSR limits. AI financial modelling for amortization schedules, wage projections, and commercial revenue must be auditable.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Auto-flags PSR threshold approaches. Tracks wage-to-revenue ratio, transfer amortization, break-even. Evidence for Premier League.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR
