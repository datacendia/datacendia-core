# Clio × Datacendia: AI Governance Scenarios

## Company Profile
- **Company:** Clio (Burnaby, BC, Canada)
- **Products:** Clio Manage, Clio Grow, Clio Duo (AI assistant), Clio Draft
- **Market:** Solo/small firm legal practice management — 150,000+ legal professionals
- **AI Products:** Clio Duo (AI-powered legal assistant), Clio Draft (AI document drafting)
- **Customers:** Solo practitioners, small firms (2-20 attorneys), mid-market firms, legal aid organisations
- **Revenue:** ~$250M+ ARR, $900M valuation
- **Datacendia Relevance:** ABA 512 applies equally to solos — but solos have NO associates to verify AI output

## Dream Team Thesis
Clio serves 150,000+ legal professionals, overwhelmingly solo and small firm practitioners. These attorneys have the LEAST capacity for manual AI supervision and the MOST exposure to malpractice. A solo using Clio Duo IS the supervising attorney, the reviewing attorney, and the filing attorney — all one person. CendiaSupervision is the second pair of eyes that solos don't have.

## SECTION A: Solo & Small Firm Practice (Scenarios 1–25)

### Scenario 1: Clio Duo — Solo Practitioner Research (Amended Statute)
**Decision Type:** General Practice
**Clio's Problem:** A solo family law attorney uses Clio Duo to research child support modification standards. The AI generates analysis citing the applicable state statute. However, the statute was amended 6 months ago — the modification threshold changed from "material change in circumstances" to "substantial change." The attorney files a motion arguing the old standard. The court denies it.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Solo supervision: Clio Duo output verified for statutory currency, amendment status confirmed, automated citation check, attorney sign-off with currency documentation.
**Applicable Regulations:** ABA Opinion 512, state family law statutes, Model Rule 1.1

### Scenario 2: Clio Duo — Deadline Calculation Error
**Decision Type:** Litigation
**Clio's Problem:** Clio Duo calculates an appeal deadline. The AI applies the standard 30-day deadline but doesn't account for a state-specific rule that excludes weekends and holidays for appeals from small claims court. The actual deadline is 30 business days, not calendar days. Appeal dismissed.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Deadline supervision: AI calculation verified per court and case type, business day vs calendar day rules confirmed, holiday exclusions applied, attorney sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** State appellate rules, FRAP (if federal), ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 3: Clio Draft — Estate Planning Document Formality
**Decision Type:** Estate Planning
**Clio's Problem:** Clio Draft generates a revocable trust document. The AI uses a template that doesn't comply with the client's state formality requirements — the state requires two witnesses AND a notary for trust amendments, while the AI-generated document only requires notarisation. A trust amendment is later challenged as improperly executed.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Document supervision: Clio Draft output verified for state-specific formality requirements, execution provisions confirmed, estate planning attorney sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** State trust law, UPC, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 4: Clio Duo — Immigration Deadline (Voluntary Departure)
**Decision Type:** Immigration
**Clio's Problem:** A solo immigration attorney manages 80 active cases using Clio Duo. The AI tracks filing deadlines. The AI misidentifies a voluntary departure deadline as 60 days when the immigration judge ordered 30 days. The client misses the deadline. Automatic removal order. 10-year re-entry bar.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Immigration supervision: AI deadline verified against IJ order, voluntary departure terms confirmed, deadline alert with order cross-check, immigration counsel sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** INA §240B, BIA rules, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 5: Clio Duo — Criminal Defence Sentencing
**Decision Type:** Criminal
**Clio's Problem:** A solo criminal defence attorney uses Clio Duo to research sentencing guidelines. The AI applies federal USSG guidelines when the case is in state court. The sentencing memorandum argues for a range that doesn't exist under state law. Strickland claim follows.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Criminal defence supervision: AI jurisdiction confirmed (state vs federal), applicable sentencing framework verified, defence counsel sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** 6th Amendment, Strickland, state sentencing guidelines, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 6: Clio Duo — Personal Injury Valuation
**Decision Type:** Personal Injury
**Clio's Problem:** Clio Duo applies a general damages multiplier from a different state. The demand letter is sent with an overvalued claim. Insurance adjuster rejects immediately. Client loses faith. Case settles for less because initial credibility was damaged.
**Datacendia's Solution:** PI supervision: AI damages methodology reviewed per jurisdiction, multiplier benchmarks confirmed, demand letter verified, PI counsel sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** State tort law, damages standards, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 7: Clio Duo — Trust Account Misclassification
**Decision Type:** Ethics
**Clio's Problem:** Clio's practice management AI misclassifies a client payment, allocating trust funds to the operating account. The error goes undetected for 3 months. Bar audit reveals the trust account deficiency. Disciplinary proceedings for commingling.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Trust account supervision: AI reconciliation verified, fund classification confirmed, trust/operating separation documented, attorney sign-off on reconciliation.
**Applicable Regulations:** Model Rule 1.15, state trust account rules, IOLTA requirements

### Scenario 8: Clio Duo — Conflict Check Failure
**Decision Type:** Ethics
**Clio's Problem:** Clio's AI-powered conflict check scans 5,000 client records. The AI misses a conflict because the adverse party's name is slightly different (Robert J. Smith vs. R. James Smith — same person). The firm takes on a conflicted matter.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Conflict supervision: AI conflict search methodology documented, fuzzy matching parameters confirmed, potential conflicts flagged for manual review, conflict counsel sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** Model Rules 1.7-1.10, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 9: Clio Duo — DUI Defence (Reversed Case)
**Decision Type:** Criminal
**Clio's Problem:** A solo DUI defence attorney uses Clio Duo to research suppression of breathalyser evidence. The AI cites a state appellate decision granting suppression — but the state supreme court reversed it 8 months ago. The motion relies on reversed authority. Motion denied. Defence collapses.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Criminal defence supervision: AI citation verified for reversal status, appellate history confirmed, current controlling authority identified, defence counsel sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** 4th Amendment, state DUI statutes, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 10: Clio Draft — LLC Operating Agreement
**Decision Type:** Corporate
**Clio's Problem:** Clio Draft generates an LLC operating agreement for a 3-member LLC. The AI uses a template with default member-managed provisions when the clients agreed to manager-managed structure. Two years later, a member dispute arises over management authority.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Formation supervision: AI template selection verified against client instructions, management structure confirmed, operating agreement reviewed, business counsel sign-off.
**Applicable Regulations:** State LLC act, ABA Opinion 512
