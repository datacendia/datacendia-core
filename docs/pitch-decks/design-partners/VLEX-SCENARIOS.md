## SECTION A: International & Mid-Market Practice (Scenarios 1–25)

### Scenario 1: Vincent AI — Multi-Jurisdiction Research Hallucination
**Decision Type:** International
**vLex's Problem:** A mid-market immigration attorney uses Vincent AI to research asylum law across 3 jurisdictions (US, UK, Canada). The AI conflates UK refugee convention interpretations with US asylum standards. The attorney relies on a UK case (presented as US authority) in a BIA brief. The immigration judge identifies the error.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Multi-jurisdiction supervision: Vincent AI output reviewed for jurisdiction accuracy, case law source confirmed per jurisdiction, immigration counsel sign-off. Evidence for BIA appeal.
**Applicable Regulations:** INA, UK Immigration Rules, IRPA (Canada), ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 2: Vincent AI — Commonwealth Case Law
**Decision Type:** Litigation
**vLex's Problem:** Vincent AI researches Australian negligence law for a cross-border product liability case. The AI cites a High Court of Australia decision that was subsequently overruled by statute. The AI's database is current on case law but missed the legislative override. The brief argues overruled law.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Commonwealth supervision: AI case law verified against legislative amendments, overrule status confirmed (case + statute), Australian solicitor sign-off. Evidence for court filing.
**Applicable Regulations:** Australian Consumer Law, ABA Opinion 512, Law Council of Australia standards

### Scenario 3: Vincent AI — Solo Practitioner Supervision
**Decision Type:** General Practice
**vLex's Problem:** A solo practitioner uses Vincent AI for a personal injury case. The attorney has no associates to verify the AI output. Vincent AI cites a state court opinion from a neighboring state — correct law, wrong jurisdiction. The filing in the correct state court misapplies the standard of care.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Solo practitioner supervision: Vincent AI output verified for jurisdictional accuracy, applicable law confirmed per forum, automated citation check, attorney sign-off. Evidence for malpractice defence.
**Applicable Regulations:** ABA Opinion 512, state bar rules, malpractice standards

### Scenario 4: Vincent AI — Latin American Legal Research
**Decision Type:** International
**vLex's Problem:** Vincent AI researches Mexican labor law for a US company expanding to Mexico. The AI applies pre-2019 Mexican labor reform provisions. The 2019 reform fundamentally changed collective bargaining, outsourcing, and worker representation. The US company's employment structure violates current Mexican law.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Latin American supervision: AI regulatory currency confirmed, reform status verified, local counsel review documented, employment counsel sign-off. Evidence for Mexican labor authority.
**Applicable Regulations:** Mexican Federal Labor Law, USMCA labor provisions, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 5: Vincent AI — Legal Aid at Scale
**Decision Type:** Access to Justice
**vLex's Problem:** A legal aid organisation deploys Vincent AI to 200 volunteer attorneys handling housing cases. The AI generates eviction defence analysis that misapplies a COVID-era moratorium that has since expired. 15 volunteer attorneys use the analysis. 15 tenants receive incorrect legal advice about their eviction protections.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Legal aid supervision: Vincent AI output verified for regulatory currency, moratorium status confirmed, batch quality control for high-volume deployment, supervisory attorney sign-off per case type. Evidence for legal aid oversight.
**Applicable Regulations:** ABA Opinion 512, legal aid standards, state tenant protection laws
