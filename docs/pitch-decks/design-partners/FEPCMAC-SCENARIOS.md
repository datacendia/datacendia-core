## SECTION A: Credit & Microfinance Decisions (Scenarios 1–40)

### Scenario 1: Microenterprise Credit Scoring AI
**Decision Type:** Credit Decision
**FEPCMAC's Problem:** Peru's Cajas use AI credit scoring models to evaluate microenterprise loans (S/. 1,000–S/. 500,000). SBS Circular B-2184 requires documented model governance. Disparate impact on indigenous and rural borrowers creates social equity risk. DS N° 115-2025-PCM mandates AI accountability.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every AI credit decision captured: model version, input features, score, outcome, adverse action reason codes in Spanish. CendiaPrecedent tracks approval rate consistency across geographic regions and demographic groups. Drift analysis detects model degradation. Regulator's Receipt exportable for SBS examination.
**Applicable Regulations:** DS N° 115-2025-PCM, SBS Circular B-2184, Ley 26702, ECOA equivalent

### Scenario 2: Individual Microloan AI (Préstamo Personal)
**Decision Type:** Credit Decision
**FEPCMAC's Problem:** Cajas issue millions of personal microloans to salaried workers and independent vendors. AI determines approval, amount, and rate. SBS requires explainability for adverse decisions. First-time borrowers with no credit history present model challenges.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures loan decisions: alternative data inputs (utility payments, mobile money, cooperative membership), AI recommendation, human officer review, override justification. Evidence for SBS model risk examination.
**Applicable Regulations:** SBS Circular B-2184, Ley 29571 (Consumer Protection), Ley 26702
