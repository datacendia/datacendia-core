## How Datacendia Helps PSG

### Scenario 1: Post-Mbappé Transfer Strategy Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**PSG's Problem:** After losing Mbappé (the most expensive player in history to recruit/retain), PSG must rebuild through smarter, AI-driven scouting rather than individual Galáctico spending. Every AI-recommended target must be defensible to QSI's sovereign wealth governance, Ligue 1 DNCG financial controls, and UEFA FSR.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every transfer input and outcome. Cryptographically signed. Evidence for QSI governance, DNCG, and UEFA.
**Applicable Regulations:** Ligue 1 DNCG, UEFA FSR, QSI sovereign governance

### Scenario 2: UEFA FFP Sanctions History
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**PSG's Problem:** PSG have been sanctioned multiple times by UEFA for FFP breaches. Future financial decisions must have audit-grade evidence demonstrating compliance. The club is under enhanced monitoring.
**Datacendia's Solution:** FinancialFairPlayDecision schema tracks all financial metrics. Auto-flags wage ratio breaches, related party issues. Regulator's Receipt provides one-click evidence for UEFA enhanced monitoring submissions.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA FSR (enhanced monitoring), Ligue 1 DNCG

### Scenario 3: Sovereign Wealth Fund Governance (QSI)
**PSG's Problem:** QSI is a sovereign wealth fund of Qatar. Sponsorship deals with Qatari entities (Qatar Airways, Qatar Tourism, QNB) are scrutinised as related party transactions. Fair market value must be demonstrable.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Auto-flags related party transactions. AI-driven sponsorship valuation with comparable deal analysis. Evidence for UEFA, DNCG, and French anti-corruption authorities.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA FSR, French anti-corruption law, Ligue 1 DNCG

### Scenario 4: French CNIL & EU AI Act Compliance
**PSG's Problem:** France's CNIL (data protection authority) is one of the most aggressive in Europe. EU AI Act applies directly to PSG's AI operations — scouting AI affecting employment decisions classified as high-risk.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway captures all AI interactions. Evidence of AI transparency, PII protection, and high-risk AI governance for CNIL and EU AI Act.
**Applicable Regulations:** GDPR, CNIL, EU AI Act (high-risk)
