## How Datacendia Helps Chelsea

### Scenario 1: £1B+ Transfer Spend Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Chelsea's Problem:** Since the Boehly/Clearlake takeover, Chelsea have spent £1B+ on transfers — the largest spend in football history over a comparable period. Long-term contracts (8+ years) are used to spread amortization, attracting PSR scrutiny. Every AI-assisted transfer valuation, contract structure, and amortization model must be defensible.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every transfer input (fee, contract length, amortization schedule, performance bonuses, sell-on clause) and outcome. Evidence of valuation methodology for Premier League, UEFA FSR, and Clearlake LP governance.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR, Clearlake LP governance

### Scenario 2: Clearlake Capital LP Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision` + Override Accountability
**Chelsea's Problem:** Clearlake Capital is a $75B+ AUM PE firm. Chelsea is an investment — LP investors demand institutional-grade governance. Every material AI-assisted decision (transfers, commercial deals, stadium) must have evidence for Clearlake quarterly LP reports.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Override accountability, drift analysis, AI governance metrics for LP reporting. Decision evidence exportable for PE due diligence when Clearlake eventually exits.
**Applicable Regulations:** US PE governance, Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 3: Multi-Club Network Governance
**Chelsea's Problem:** Chelsea own RC Strasbourg (France) and Vitória de Guimarães (Portugal). Player loans between clubs must be at arm's length. AI-driven player movement, loan valuations, and development decisions across the network face related-party scrutiny from the Premier League, Ligue 1, and Liga Portugal.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Cross-jurisdiction conflict detection for player movements. Evidence of arm's-length loan valuations. CendiaPrecedent compares inter-network valuations against external market rates.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League multi-club rules, Ligue 1 DNCG, Liga Portugal, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 4: Amortization Strategy Evidence
**Chelsea's Problem:** Chelsea's 8+ year contracts spread transfer fees over longer periods, reducing annual PSR charges. This strategy is novel and may be challenged by the Premier League. AI-driven amortization modelling must be defensible as legitimate accounting, not PSR manipulation.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for every amortization model — methodology, comparable analysis, accounting standards compliance. Regulator's Receipt for Premier League if the strategy is challenged.
**Applicable Regulations:** IFRS, Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 5: Stamford Bridge Redevelopment

**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League medical regulations, UK duty of care law

**Applicable Regulations:** FIFA Article 19, UK safeguarding law, FA licensing
