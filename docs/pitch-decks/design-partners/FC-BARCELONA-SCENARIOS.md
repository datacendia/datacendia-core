## How Datacendia Helps Barcelona

### Scenario 1: Player Transfer Governance Under Socios Scrutiny
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Barça's Problem:** As the world's largest member-owned club, every transfer must be defensible to 160,000+ socios who can vote the president out. After the €1.5B debt crisis, AI-driven scouting and salary decisions face extraordinary transparency demands.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every transfer input and outcome with cryptographic signatures. Produces evidence packets that demonstrate due diligence — exportable for socios assemblies, La Liga FFP, and UEFA FSR.
**Applicable Regulations:** La Liga FFP, UEFA FSR, Spanish corporate law, socios governance

### Scenario 2: Financial Restructuring & "Lever" Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Barça's Problem:** Barcelona activated financial "levers" — selling future revenue streams (TV rights, merchandising, Barça Studios) to generate immediate liquidity. AI-driven financial modelling for lever valuations must be auditable for socios, La Liga, and Spanish regulators.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Tracks revenue, wages, related party transactions, owner injections. Every AI-assisted financial projection has a decision receipt with full reasoning chain. Evidence for La Liga economic control, UEFA FSR, and socios transparency.
**Applicable Regulations:** La Liga economic control, UEFA FSR, Spanish corporate law

### Scenario 3: Barça Innovation Hub AI Governance
**Barça's Problem:** The Innovation Hub uses AI across player development, sports science, biomechanics, and tactical analysis. These AI models influence decisions about La Masia graduates — who gets promoted, who is released. Under Spanish employment law and EU AI Act (high-risk for employment decisions), these AI-assisted personnel decisions need governance.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway wraps Innovation Hub AI outputs. Every recommendation receives a decision receipt. Evidence of AI governance for EU AI Act compliance.
**Applicable Regulations:** EU AI Act (high-risk), Spanish employment law

### Scenario 4: La Masia Youth Development & Safeguarding
**Decision Type:** `YouthDevelopmentDecision`
**Barça's Problem:** La Masia is football's most famous academy (Messi, Xavi, Iniesta). Barcelona recruit internationally — FIFA sanctioned Barça in 2014 for violating Article 19 (international transfer of minors). Cannot happen again.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every academy intake: DBS/background checks, parental consent, education plans, training hours, wellbeing. Auto-checks FIFA Article 19 before international recruitment proceeds. Audit-grade evidence for FIFA, RFEF, and Spanish child protection authorities.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIFA Article 19, Spanish child protection law, RFEF licensing

### Scenario 5: Spotify Camp Nou Renovation (€1.5B Project)
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`
**Barça's Problem:** The Camp Nou is undergoing a €1.5B renovation (Espai Barça). AI-driven project management, capacity modelling, commercial projections, and safety planning for a 105,000-capacity stadium. Socios must approve via referendum.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures venue decisions, safety assessments, capacity changes, accessibility compliance. Evidence trail for Barcelona City Council, socios assembly, and project financiers.
**Applicable Regulations:** Spanish planning law, safety regulations, socios referendum requirements
