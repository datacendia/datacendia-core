## How Datacendia Helps Atletico Madrid

### Scenario 1: Transfer Arbitrage Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Atletico's Problem:** Atletico's competitive edge is scouting efficiency — buying undervalued talent, developing under Simeone, and selling at profit. This model requires precise AI-driven player valuations at both buy and sell points. Every valuation must be defensible to Quantum Pacific's governance, La Liga FFP, and UEFA FSR.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures buy-side inputs (undervaluation metrics, comparable deals, performance projections, development timeline) and sell-side outcomes (market timing, peak valuation evidence, agent fee analysis). CendiaPrecedent tracks arbitrage success rate over time. Evidence for Quantum Pacific, La Liga, and UEFA.
**Applicable Regulations:** La Liga FFP, UEFA FSR, Spanish corporate law
