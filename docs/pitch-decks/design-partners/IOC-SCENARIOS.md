## How Datacendia Helps the IOC

### Scenario 1: AI-Assisted Judging Evidence (Gymnastics, Diving, Figure Skating)
**Decision Type:** Custom — Judging Decision
**IOC's Problem:** The IOC and international federations (FIG, FINA, ISU) are introducing AI-assisted judging in gymnastics (Fujitsu AI scoring), diving, and figure skating. AI scores affect Olympic medal outcomes — the highest-stakes decisions in sport. Athletes from 206 nations challenge scores through appeal processes. Currently no cryptographic evidence of AI model inputs, confidence levels, or calibration.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every AI-assisted judging decision captured: sensor inputs, biomechanical analysis, difficulty assessment, execution scoring, confidence levels, human judge comparison. Cryptographically hashed and timestamped. Athletes can request a Regulator's Receipt for any score. Evidence for CAS appeals.
**Applicable Regulations:** Olympic Charter, International Federation technical rules, CAS

### Scenario 2: Anti-Doping — WADA Global Partnership
**Decision Type:** `AntiDopingDecision`
**IOC's Problem:** The IOC/WADA anti-doping programme tests 5,000+ athletes per Olympics. AI-driven sample analysis, biological passport monitoring, and pattern detection. The Russian doping scandal (2015-ongoing) demonstrated that anti-doping evidence must be tamper-proof. China's 23 positive tests controversy (2024) showed evidence handling failures.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Complete chain-of-custody for every test — sample collection, transport, lab analysis, results management. Cryptographic hashing proves samples weren't tampered with. Evidence for CAS, WADA, and national anti-doping agencies.
**Applicable Regulations:** WADA Code, Olympic Charter, CAS

### Scenario 3: Host City Selection Governance
**IOC's Problem:** Olympic host city selection was historically corruption-prone (Salt Lake City 2002 scandal). The reformed dual-track process uses AI-driven feasibility analysis. Every assessment must demonstrate integrity for IOC Ethics Commission and public trust.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for every host city assessment — technical evaluation, financial feasibility, human rights assessment. Regulator's Receipt for IOC Ethics Commission.
**Applicable Regulations:** Olympic Charter, IOC Ethics Code, host city contract

### Scenario 4: Broadcast AI Governance ($7.6B Revenue)
**IOC's Problem:** Olympic broadcast revenue ($7.6B/Olympiad) is the IOC's primary income. AI-driven broadcast production (automated camera selection, highlight generation, personalised streaming) must be transparent. EU AI Act applies to consumer-facing AI.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway captures all broadcast AI interactions. Evidence of AI transparency for EU AI Act.
**Applicable Regulations:** EU AI Act, Swiss FADP, broadcast agreements

### Scenario 5: Athlete Eligibility AI (Gender, Nationality, Age)
**IOC's Problem:** AI-driven eligibility decisions (gender eligibility frameworks, nationality changes, age verification) are among the most controversial in sport. Each decision affects individual athletes' dreams and involves human rights considerations.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every eligibility decision captured with full reasoning, medical evidence (where applicable), and human rights assessment. Evidence for CAS appeals.
**Applicable Regulations:** Olympic Charter, international human rights law, CAS

### Scenario 6: Revenue Distribution to 206 NOCs
**IOC's Problem:** The IOC distributes billions to 206 NOCs, international federations, and Olympic Solidarity. AI-driven allocation models must be transparent and consistent.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for every allocation. CendiaPrecedent tracks consistency across NOCs.
**Applicable Regulations:** Olympic Charter, Swiss financial oversight
