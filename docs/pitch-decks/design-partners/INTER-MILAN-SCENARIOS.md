## How Datacendia Helps Inter Milan

### Scenario 1: Oaktree Capital Debt Restructuring Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision` + Override Accountability
**Inter's Problem:** Oaktree Capital (a $190B AUM US hedge fund) took control of Inter in 2024 after Suning defaulted on a €395M Goldman Sachs loan. Every financial decision — transfers, wages, commercial deals — must satisfy Oaktree's LP governance, debt covenants, and demonstrate a path to financial sustainability. AI-driven financial modelling for debt restructuring, cash flow projections, and cost optimisation must be institutional-grade.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for every AI-driven financial projection. Override accountability when management deviates from AI cost-cutting recommendations. Drift analysis tracks financial health over time. Evidence for Oaktree LP reports, bond covenant compliance, and potential future sale due diligence.
**Applicable Regulations:** US hedge fund governance, FIGC, UEFA FSR, Italian corporate law

### Scenario 2: Transfer Budget Constraints
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Inter's Problem:** Under Oaktree, Inter operate with minimal net transfer spending. AI-driven scouting must identify undervalued targets and optimise sell-on revenues. Every transfer must be justified within strict financial parameters.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures transfer inputs with financial constraint compliance. Auto-flags when proposed deals breach budget ceilings. CendiaPrecedent validates that transfers under financial constraints consistently produce value.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIGC, UEFA FSR, Oaktree financial parameters

### Scenario 3: New Stadium / San Siro Shared Decision
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`
**Inter's Problem:** Inter and AC Milan jointly plan a new €1.3B stadium to replace San Siro. AI-driven feasibility, revenue modelling, and cost allocation between two rival clubs sharing a project. Each club's AI projections must be independently verifiable.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures venue decisions with evidence of fair cost allocation between co-developers. Revenue projections with documented methodology. Evidence for Milan municipality, both clubs' boards, and project financiers.
**Applicable Regulations:** Italian planning law, Milan municipal, EU construction

### Scenario 4: Financial Fair Play Compliance
