## How Datacendia Helps Brentford

### Scenario 1: SmartOdds-Integrated Recruitment Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Brentford's Problem:** Matthew Benham founded SmartOdds (betting analytics consultancy) before buying Brentford. The club's recruitment model is built on SmartOdds-derived analytics. Like Brighton/Bloom, this creates a gambling/football governance question — AI models must demonstrate independence from betting operations.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence proves separation between SmartOdds betting analytics and Brentford's recruitment AI. Every recommendation has a decision receipt with methodology, data sources, and compliance mapping. Evidence for FA integrity.
**Applicable Regulations:** FA gambling regulations, Gambling Commission, Premier League PSR

### Scenario 2: Multi-Club Governance (FC Midtjylland)
**Brentford's Problem:** Benham also owns FC Midtjylland (Denmark). Player movements, loans, and shared analytics between clubs face related-party scrutiny from the Premier League, Danish Superliga, and UEFA.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Cross-jurisdiction conflict detection between English and Danish regulatory requirements. Evidence of arm's-length dealing for inter-club transactions.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League multi-club rules, Danish FA, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 3: B-Team Model Governance (No Academy)
**Decision Type:** `YouthDevelopmentDecision`
**Brentford's Problem:** Brentford controversially closed their academy and operate a B-team instead — signing players aged 17-21 rather than developing from childhood. This model relies heavily on AI-driven identification of late-development talent. Every B-team signing must be defensible as a football decision, not an exploitation of young players outside academy protections.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures B-team recruitment decisions with age-appropriate duty of care evidence. Evidence of proper development pathways, education support, and welfare monitoring even without academy classification.
**Applicable Regulations:** FA regulations, UK employment law (young workers), Premier League

### Scenario 4: Small-Stadium Premier League Survival
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Brentford's Problem:** Gtech Community Stadium (17,250) is by far the smallest in the Premier League. Matchday revenue is capped — AI-driven financial modelling must optimise commercial revenue, transfer timing, and wage management within tight constraints.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Auto-flags financial constraint breaches. CendiaPrecedent tracks value-for-money across constrained budgets.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR
