## How Datacendia Helps Manchester City / CFG

### Scenario 1: 115 Premier League Charges — AI Evidence Defence
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision` + Regulator's Receipt
**City's Problem:** Manchester City face 115 charges from the Premier League for alleged breaches of financial rules spanning 2009-2023 — the most serious regulatory threat in Premier League history. If City had Datacendia-grade evidence for every financial decision, they could produce cryptographic proof of compliance for each charge.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Retroactively, Datacendia can't help. Prospectively, every AI-driven financial decision (transfer valuation, related party transaction, sponsorship valuation) produces a Regulator's Receipt with Merkle root verification, cryptographic hashing, and compliance mapping — exactly the evidence needed to defend against such charges.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League financial rules, UEFA FSR, CAS

**Decision Type:** All sports decision types
**City's Problem:** CFG own 13 clubs across 5 continents (Man City, Melbourne City, New York City FC, Mumbai City FC, Yokohama F. Marinos, etc.).
