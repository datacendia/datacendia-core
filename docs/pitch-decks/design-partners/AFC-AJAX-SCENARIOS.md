## How Datacendia Helps Ajax

### Scenario 1: Academy-to-Sale Pipeline Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision` + `YouthDevelopmentDecision`
**Ajax's Problem:** Ajax's entire model is academy development → first team → sale to elite clubs. De Jong (€75M to Barça), de Ligt (€75M to Juventus), Antony (€100M to Man Utd). AI-driven development tracking, promotion decisions, and sell-timing must satisfy AFM (Dutch securities regulator), KNVB, UEFA FSR, and Euronext shareholders.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures youth development decisions (promotion criteria, training plans, education compliance) and sell-side decisions (market timing, comparable analysis, agent fee compliance). CendiaPrecedent tracks academy-to-sale ROI over decades.
**Applicable Regulations:** AFM, KNVB licensing, UEFA FSR, Euronext listing rules

### Scenario 2: Euronext Amsterdam / AFM Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Ajax's Problem:** As a Euronext-listed club, Ajax must provide transparent financial reporting. Player sales are material events requiring timely disclosure. AI-driven valuations must be audit-grade for AFM.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Override accountability, AI governance metrics for annual reports and AFM compliance.
**Applicable Regulations:** AFM, Dutch Corporate Governance Code, Euronext

### Scenario 3: Johan Cruyff Legacy — Development Philosophy Governance
**Ajax's Problem:** Ajax's development philosophy (Cruyff Plan) is systematised. AI-augmented development decisions must align with the philosophy — not just optimise for sale value. The tension between development philosophy and commercial optimisation needs documented governance.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence captures both development philosophy alignment AND commercial metrics. CendiaPrecedent flags when decisions prioritise sale value over development.
