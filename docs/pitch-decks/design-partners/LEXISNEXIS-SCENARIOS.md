### Scenario 1: Lexis+ AI Brief Research Supervision
**Decision Type:** Litigation
**Thomson Reuters' Problem (LexisNexis equivalent):** A litigation partner uses Lexis+ AI to research a novel constitutional question. The AI generates a persuasive analysis citing 7 cases. Two of the cited cases are real but mischaracterised — the holdings are stated backwards. The brief is filed. Opposing counsel's Shepard's check reveals the mischaracterisation. **Datacendia's Solution:** Legal research supervision: every Lexis+ AI citation verified against Shepard's, holding characterisation confirmed, supervising attorney sign-off. Evidence for FRCP Rule 11 compliance.
**Applicable Regulations:** ABA Opinion 512, FRCP Rule 11, Model Rules 1.1, 3.3
