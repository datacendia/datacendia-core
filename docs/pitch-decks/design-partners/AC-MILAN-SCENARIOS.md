## How Datacendia Helps AC Milan

### Scenario 1: RedBird Capital LP Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision` + `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Milan's Problem:** RedBird Capital Partners acquired Milan for €1.2B in 2022. As a US private equity firm, RedBird must report AI-assisted investment decisions to their Limited Partners (LPs). Every transfer, commercial deal, and stadium investment is part of RedBird's fund returns. LP governance demands institutional-grade evidence.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Override accountability, drift analysis, and AI governance metrics exportable for RedBird LP quarterly reports. Every transfer has a decision receipt with full valuation methodology, comparable analysis, and compliance mapping.
**Applicable Regulations:** US PE governance standards, FIGC, UEFA FSR, Italian corporate law

### Scenario 2: Milan Lab Sports Science Legacy
**Milan's Problem:** Milan Lab (est. 2002) was one of the first sports science units using predictive analytics for injury prevention and player longevity (Maldini played until 41). Under RedBird, Milan Lab's AI models are being modernised. These AI-driven medical and performance decisions affect player welfare and transfer valuations.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway wraps Milan Lab AI outputs with evidence capture. Every injury prediction, load management recommendation, and return-to-play decision gets a decision receipt. Evidence of AI governance for EU AI Act (high-risk medical AI).
**Applicable Regulations:** EU AI Act (high-risk), Italian medical law, FIGC

### Scenario 3: New Stadium Project (€1.3B)
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`
**Milan's Problem:** Milan (and Inter) plan to leave San Siro for a new €1.3B stadium. AI-driven site selection, capacity modelling, commercial projections, and environmental impact assessments. Milan municipality approval required.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every venue decision: site analysis, capacity, safety, accessibility, commercial projections, environmental impact. Evidence for Milan municipality, project lenders, and RedBird LPs.
**Applicable Regulations:** Italian planning law, Milan municipal regulations, EU environmental standards

### Scenario 4: Financial Fair Play Compliance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Milan's Problem:** After years of financial instability (Chinese ownership, Elliott Management receivership), Milan under RedBird must demonstrate sustained financial health to FIGC and UEFA.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Auto-flags financial ratio breaches. Tracks wage-to-revenue, amortization, break-even. Evidence for FIGC and UEFA FSR.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIGC financial rules, UEFA FSR
