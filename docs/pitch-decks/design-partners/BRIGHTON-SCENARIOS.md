## How Datacendia Helps Brighton

### Scenario 1: When AI IS the Competitive Strategy — Model Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Brighton's Problem:** Brighton's entire competitive edge is AI-driven scouting. Tony Bloom's StarLizard background means the club's recruitment model is more sophisticated than most hedge funds. But when AI is your strategy, governance of that AI is existential — a model failure doesn't just cost money, it costs league position and Premier League survival. Who audits the AI that runs the club?
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway wraps Brighton's proprietary AI models with evidence capture. Every recommendation gets a decision receipt. Drift analysis tracks model accuracy over time. CendiaPrecedent compares recommendations against outcomes. Evidence of AI model governance for EU AI Act (high-risk employment decisions) and Premier League PSR.
**Applicable Regulations:** EU AI Act (high-risk), Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 2: Sell-Side Valuation Evidence
**Brighton's Problem:** Brighton's model produces massive sell-side returns — Caicedo (bought ~£5M, sold £115M), Mac Allister (bought £7M, sold £35M), Cucurella (bought £15M, sold £62M). Each sale requires defensible valuation evidence — why sell now? Why this price? Could they have gotten more?
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures sell-timing inputs (market conditions, comparable deals, player peak-value modelling) and outcomes. CendiaPrecedent validates that sell-timing consistently maximises value.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 3: Tony Bloom — Gambling/Football Conflict Governance
**Brighton's Problem:** Tony Bloom is a professional gambler who owns a football club. The FA's gambling regulations require strict separation between betting activities and football operations. AI-driven decisions at Brighton must demonstrate zero influence from Bloom's gambling operations.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence proves separation — AI models used for recruitment are distinct from and uninfluenced by gambling models. Audit trail for FA integrity.
**Applicable Regulations:** FA gambling regulations, Gambling Commission, UEFA integrity

### Scenario 4: Manager Transition Resilience
**Brighton's Problem:** Brighton have lost three managers in quick succession (Potter → De Zerbi → Hürzeler) as larger clubs poach them. The AI model must be manager-proof — consistently delivering regardless of who manages. Evidence of this resilience validates the model.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaPrecedent tracks recruitment quality across manager transitions. "The model identified Mitoma under Manager A, Mac Allister under Manager B, and Target X under Manager C — consistent quality regardless of manager."
