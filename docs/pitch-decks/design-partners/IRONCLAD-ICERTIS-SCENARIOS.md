- **Customers:** Fortune 500 enterprises, mid-market companies, procurement departments, legal operations
- **Combined Revenue:** ~$500M+ ARR
- **Datacendia Relevance:** Contract AI without supervision layer — AI reviews and approves contracts without documented attorney oversight

## Dream Team Thesis
CLM platforms process millions of contracts annually. When AI reviews a contract and flags (or misses) a risk, that determination flows into business decisions worth billions. Contract AI without documented human supervision creates enterprise-wide liability exposure. CendiaSupervision is the governance layer between AI contract analysis and business action.

## SECTION A: Contract Review & Generation (Scenarios 1–25)

### Scenario 1: Ironclad AI — Missed Indemnification Cap
**Decision Type:** Contract
**Ironclad's Problem:** Ironclad AI reviews a vendor agreement and classifies the indemnification provision as "standard." The AI misses that the indemnification cap is set at 1x annual fees (standard) BUT the carve-outs for IP infringement and data breach are UNCAPPED — creating unlimited liability exposure. The contract is approved via automated workflow without attorney review of the carve-outs.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Contract supervision: AI risk classification reviewed, indemnification structure confirmed (cap + carve-outs), material risk provisions flagged for attorney review, procurement counsel sign-off. Evidence for contract dispute.
**Applicable Regulations:** UCC, contract law, ABA Opinion 512, corporate governance

### Scenario 2: Icertis AI — Auto-Renewal Trap
**Decision Type:** Contract
**Icertis' Problem:** Icertis ExploreAI extracts key terms from 5,000 vendor contracts. The AI correctly identifies auto-renewal clauses in 4,920 contracts (98.4%). But it misses 80 contracts with auto-renewal provisions embedded in non-standard locations (appendices, SLAs, order forms). 23 of those contracts auto-renew before the client can terminate. Lock-in cost: $3.4M.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Extraction supervision: AI term extraction with QC sampling across document sections, auto-renewal detection verified in all document components, contract operations sign-off. Evidence for termination dispute.
**Applicable Regulations:** UCC, contract law, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 3: Ironclad AI — AI-Generated Contract Clause
**Decision Type:** Contract
**Ironclad's Problem:** Ironclad AI generates a limitation of liability clause for a SaaS agreement. The AI-generated clause limits liability to "direct damages" but uses language that a court in the target jurisdiction interprets as also waiving consequential damages for data breach — which the client intended to preserve.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Contract generation supervision: AI-generated clauses verified for jurisdictional interpretation, unintended waivers flagged, governing law implications confirmed, technology counsel sign-off. Evidence for contract dispute.
**Applicable Regulations:** UCC §2-719, state contract law, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 4: Icertis AI — Obligation Management Failure
**Decision Type:** Contract
**Icertis' Problem:** Icertis obligation management AI tracks 12,000 contractual obligations across a Fortune 500 company. The AI misclassifies a GDPR data processing obligation as a "reporting" obligation rather than a "compliance" obligation, resulting in lower priority treatment. The company misses a DPA-mandated audit deadline. GDPR fine: €1.8M.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Obligation supervision: AI classification reviewed per regulation, obligation priority confirmed, compliance deadline verified, DPO/compliance officer sign-off. Evidence for DPA response.
**Applicable Regulations:** GDPR, DPA enforcement, corporate governance

### Scenario 5: Ironclad AI — M&A Contract Migration
**Decision Type:** Corporate
**Ironclad's Problem:** After a $2B acquisition, Ironclad AI analyses 8,000 target company contracts for change of control provisions. The AI identifies 340 contracts requiring consent. Post-closing, 12 additional contracts with CoC provisions are discovered — the AI missed non-standard language. 3 material vendors terminate.
**Datacendia's Solution:** M&A migration supervision: AI contract analysis with statistical QC sampling, CoC detection verified across language variants, deal integration counsel sign-off. Evidence for R&W claim.
**Applicable Regulations:** ABA Opinion 512, R&W insurance standards, corporate governance
