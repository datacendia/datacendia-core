**Decision Type:** `BiasMitigationDecision`

**Datacendia's Solution:** Real-time cognitive bias detection in matchmaking: when a proposed fight has both high commercial value AND elevated safety risk, the system flags the potential bias and forces documented acknowledgment before proceeding. Counter-suggestion generation proposes alternative matchups that maintain commercial appeal with reduced safety risk. Override accountability ensures that if a matchmaker proceeds despite a safety flag, the commercial rationale and safety risk acceptance are documented — creating evidence that the decision was conscious, not negligent. Historical pattern analysis detects if certain fighter demographics or weight classes systematically have safety flags overridden more often (indicating structural bias). Evidence for duty of care defence, state athletic commission matchmaking review, and antitrust litigation (safety-first matchmaking refutes the "profit over safety" narrative).
