## How Datacendia Helps World Rugby

### Scenario 1: Concussion Protocol Evidence (The Existential Issue)
**Decision Type:** Custom — Player Safety / Medical Decision
**World Rugby's Problem:** Rugby faces the most acute concussion/CTE crisis in professional sport. A class action lawsuit by 300+ former players (including Steve Thompson, 2003 World Cup winner who can't remember winning) alleges World Rugby failed to protect players from brain injury. Every Head Injury Assessment (HIA) and return-to-play decision could become a legal exhibit. AI-driven head impact detection (instrumented mouthguards, video analysis) produces recommendations — but without evidence infrastructure, these AI recommendations have no legal standing.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every HIA decision captured: impact data (mouthguard sensors — force, direction, duration), video analysis, medical assessment, return-to-play recommendation, independent doctor sign-off. Hard-stop guardrail blocks return-to-play without clearance. Cryptographic evidence chain for litigation. Court bundle export for class action defence.
**Applicable Regulations:** World Rugby Concussion Guidelines, national duty of care law, CTE litigation

### Scenario 2: Instrumented Mouthguard AI Governance
**World Rugby's Problem:** World Rugby mandates instrumented mouthguards in elite competitions — AI analyses head impact data in real-time. But the AI's accuracy, false positive/negative rates, and calibration must be defensible in court. If the mouthguard AI says "low risk" and the player later develops CTE, World Rugby needs evidence of the AI's methodology.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway wraps mouthguard AI outputs with evidence capture. Every impact assessment has a decision receipt with sensor data, AI model version, confidence score, and calibration status. Drift analysis tracks model accuracy over time.
**Applicable Regulations:** Medical device regulations, EU AI Act (high-risk medical AI), product liability

### Scenario 3: Player Welfare Longitudinal Tracking
**World Rugby's Problem:** CTE develops over years/decades. World Rugby needs longitudinal evidence that player welfare decisions were consistently sound across entire careers — not just individual matches.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaPrecedent tracks welfare decisions across a player's career. "Player X received 47 head impacts across 8 seasons — every HIA was governed, every return-to-play was medically cleared." Evidence for lifetime welfare claims.
**Applicable Regulations:** Duty of care, employer liability, insurance requirements

### Scenario 4: Insurance Cost Crisis
**World Rugby's Problem:** Rugby insurance premiums are spiralling due to CTE litigation risk. Insurers demand evidence of risk management. Without AI governance evidence, premiums will become unaffordable — threatening rugby's viability at all levels.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI Insurance Evidence packages demonstrate comprehensive risk management. Evidence of every safety decision reduces premiums. Regulator's Receipt for insurers.
**Applicable Regulations:** Insurance regulatory requirements, actuarial evidence standards

### Scenario 5: Anti-Doping
**Decision Type:** `AntiDopingDecision`
**Datacendia's Solution:** Chain-of-custody for WADA testing across 130+ unions.
**Applicable Regulations:** WADA Code, national anti-doping agencies

### Scenario 6: Match Integrity
