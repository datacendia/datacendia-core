## How Datacendia Helps Rangers

### Scenario 1: Post-Liquidation Financial Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Rangers' Problem:** Rangers (oldco) were liquidated in 2012 due to HMRC tax arrears and financial mismanagement — the biggest corporate failure in Scottish football history. The newco rebuilt from the fourth tier. Financial governance is not a nice-to-have; it's existential. Every AI-assisted financial decision must demonstrate the lessons of 2012 have been learned. Shareholders, the SFA, and UEFA must see audit-grade evidence of financial discipline.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every financial decision produces a Regulator's Receipt. Auto-flags when spending approaches dangerous levels. Override accountability captures when management deviates from AI financial recommendations — with full justification documented. Drift analysis shows longitudinal financial health.
**Applicable Regulations:** SFA licensing, UEFA club licensing, AIM listing rules, UK Companies Act

### Scenario 2: AIM Stock Exchange Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision` + Override Accountability
**Rangers' Problem:** Rangers International Football Club plc is listed on AIM (London Stock Exchange's junior market). While lighter than a full LSE listing, AIM still requires proper corporate governance, timely disclosure of material events (player transfers are material), and transparent financial reporting.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI governance metrics for annual reports and AIM compliance. Decision evidence for shareholder AGMs.
**Applicable Regulations:** AIM Rules, FCA (Financial Conduct Authority), UK Corporate Governance Code

### Scenario 3: Old Firm Competitive Parity
**Rangers' Problem:** The Old Firm rivalry with Celtic is existential to both clubs. Celtic plc are publicly traded and investing heavily. Rangers must match Celtic's investment while maintaining financial discipline — the lesson of 2012. AI-driven scouting must maximise value to compete with Celtic's budget.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaPrecedent benchmarks Rangers' transfer value-for-money against Celtic and European peers. Evidence of competitive spending efficiency.

### Scenario 4: European Competition Governance
**Rangers' Problem:** Rangers reached the 2022 Europa League final (Seville). European competition brings UEFA club licensing, UEFA FSR compliance, and higher-profile integrity monitoring.
