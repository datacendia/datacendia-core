## How Datacendia Helps UEFA

### Scenario 1: Financial Sustainability Regulations (FSR) Enforcement
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**UEFA's Problem:** UEFA's Club Financial Control Body (CFCB) assesses 700+ clubs annually under the new FSR framework. AI-driven financial analysis evaluates wage-to-revenue ratios (70% threshold), break-even compliance, overdue payables, and related party transactions. When UEFA sanctions a club (transfer ban, fine, point deduction, exclusion), the club appeals to CAS — and UEFA must prove its AI-assisted financial analysis was sound.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every CFCB AI-assisted assessment produces a Regulator's Receipt with full financial analysis methodology, data inputs, comparable club analysis, and compliance determination. Evidence for CAS appeals — UEFA proves its assessment methodology was fair, consistent, and based on verified data.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA FSR, CAS jurisprudence, Swiss association law

### Scenario 2: Club Licensing — 700+ Clubs Annually
**UEFA's Problem:** UEFA club licensing determines which clubs can participate in European competition. AI-driven screening of financial submissions, sporting criteria, infrastructure, and legal compliance across 55 member associations. Consistency of decisions must be demonstrable — a club denied a licence in one association should be treated the same way in another.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaPrecedent tracks licensing decisions across 55 associations. Flags when similar clubs receive different outcomes. Evidence for consistency reporting and CAS appeals.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA Club Licensing Regulations, CAS

### Scenario 3: Champions League VAR Governance
**Decision Type:** Custom — Officiating Decision
**UEFA's Problem:** VAR in the Champions League affects €2B+ in prize money distribution. A VAR call that eliminates a club from the competition has direct financial consequences. Clubs increasingly challenge VAR methodology.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every VAR decision captured: video inputs, referee decision process, VAR recommendation, final outcome, confidence metrics. Regulator's Receipt for clubs requesting VAR evidence.
**Applicable Regulations:** IFAB Laws of the Game, UEFA VAR Protocol

### Scenario 4: Match Integrity — European Competition
**Decision Type:** `MatchIntegrityDecision`
**UEFA's Problem:** UEFA's integrity programme monitors betting patterns across Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, and qualifying rounds. AI-driven anomaly detection flags suspicious matches. Investigation evidence must satisfy CAS and national law enforcement.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures betting anomaly alerts, investigation process, intelligence sharing, law enforcement referrals. Evidence for UEFA Disciplinary Committee, CAS, and national police.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA Disciplinary Regulations, national criminal law, Interpol

### Scenario 5: Financial Distribution — Solidarity & Prize Money
**UEFA's Problem:** UEFA distributes €3.5B+ per Champions League cycle across participating clubs, relegated clubs (solidarity), and non-participating clubs (youth development). AI-driven allocation models must be transparent and auditable.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for every allocation. CendiaPrecedent tracks consistency across cycles. Evidence for member associations and club representatives.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA distribution regulations, EU competition law

### Scenario 6: Related Party Transaction Assessment
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**UEFA's Problem:** The most contentious area of FSR: assessing whether sponsorship deals involving club owners (e.g., Etihad-Man City, QTA-PSG, Emirates-Real Madrid) are at fair market value. AI-driven fair value assessment of related party deals is central to FSR enforcement — and the primary basis for CAS appeals.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI-driven sponsorship valuation with comparable deal analysis, market benchmarking, and independent valuation methodology. Every assessment produces a Regulator's Receipt. Evidence for CAS when clubs challenge fair value determinations.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA FSR, CAS, EU competition law

### Scenario 7: Anti-Doping — European Competition
**Decision Type:** `AntiDopingDecision`
**UEFA's Problem:** UEFA manages anti-doping for all European competitions. Chain of custody for testing across 55 associations.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Chain-of-custody evidence at European scale. Evidence for WADA, CAS.
**Applicable Regulations:** WADA Code, UEFA Anti-Doping Regulations

### Scenario 8: Stadium Safety — European Competition Venues
