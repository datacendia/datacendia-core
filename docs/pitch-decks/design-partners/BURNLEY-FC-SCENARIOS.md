### Scenario 1: Post-Relegation Financial Governance — Parachute Payment Management
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**Burnley's Problem:** Relegation triggers a financial cliff: revenue drops from ~£170M to ~£50M while player contracts remain at Premier League levels. **Datacendia's Solution:** Every financial projection sealed with AI model assumptions. Scenario modelling (promotion Y1, Y2, no promotion Y3) with break-even analysis. Evidence for EFL P&S assessment and ALK Capital investor reporting.
**Applicable Regulations:** EFL P&S Rules, EFL STCC, Companies Act 2006, ALK Capital LP governance
