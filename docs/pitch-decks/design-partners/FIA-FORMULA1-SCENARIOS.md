## How Datacendia Helps FIA / Formula 1

### Scenario 1: Cost Cap Enforcement ($140M)
**Decision Type:** Custom — Financial Compliance
**F1's Problem:** The $140M cost cap (introduced 2021) fundamentally changed F1. Red Bull breached the cap in 2022 (minor, £1.8M overspend). AI-driven cost cap monitoring must verify 10 teams' financial submissions annually — each worth hundreds of millions. Teams challenge FIA assessments at CAS.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every cost cap assessment produces a Regulator's Receipt with full financial analysis methodology, line-item review, and comparable analysis. Evidence for CAS when teams appeal. CendiaPrecedent ensures consistent treatment across all 10 teams.
**Applicable Regulations:** F1 Financial Regulations, CAS, FIA International Sporting Code

### Scenario 2: AI Race Strategy Governance
**F1's Problem:** Every F1 team uses AI for race strategy — tire degradation models, pit window optimization, weather prediction, safety car probability. These AI decisions directly affect race outcomes worth $200M+ in prize money. When a strategy fails, teams question whether the AI was properly governed. FIA may eventually regulate team AI systems.
**Datacendia's Solution:** CendiaGateway captures team AI strategy interactions. Decision evidence for every strategic call — why pit now? Why stay out? Evidence of AI model governance for FIA and team stakeholders.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIA Sporting Regulations, team governance

### Scenario 3: Technical Regulation Enforcement
**F1's Problem:** FIA scrutineers use AI-assisted analysis to verify car legality — aerodynamic compliance, engine power unit regulations, fuel flow monitoring. A car deemed illegal after a race results in disqualification (Alpine/Aston Martin fuel temperature DQs). AI-assisted technical rulings must be evidence-grade.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every technical assessment captured with sensor data, analysis methodology, and compliance determination. Evidence for FIA International Court of Appeal.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIA Technical Regulations, FIA International Sporting Code

### Scenario 4: Driver Safety Systems (Halo, Barriers, Medical)
**Decision Type:** Custom — Safety Decision
**F1's Problem:** F1 safety systems (halo, barrier design, medical car deployment) are AI-optimised. Circuit design uses AI-driven crash simulation. Every safety decision is life-or-death — Romain Grosjean's 2020 Bahrain crash survival validated the halo. AI-driven safety recommendations must have court-admissible evidence for liability.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Hard-stop guardrails for safety non-compliance. Every safety recommendation captured with simulation data, risk assessment, and engineering evidence. Evidence for FIA Safety Commission, circuit liability, and national regulatory authorities.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIA safety regulations, national circuit licensing, liability law

### Scenario 5: Liberty Media NYSE Governance
**F1's Problem:** F1 is owned by Liberty Media (NYSE:FWONA). AI-assisted commercial decisions (race calendar, broadcast deals, sponsorship) are material events requiring SOX-compliant governance for SEC filings.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI governance metrics exportable for Liberty Media annual reports and SEC compliance.
**Applicable Regulations:** SEC, SOX, NYSE listing rules

### Scenario 6: Race Stewards / Penalty Governance
**F1's Problem:** Race stewards impose penalties (time penalties, grid drops, disqualifications) that determine championship outcomes. Inconsistent steward decisions are F1's most contentious issue. AI-assisted steward analysis (incident reconstruction, telemetry review) needs evidence.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every steward decision captured with telemetry data, video analysis, precedent comparison. CendiaPrecedent flags inconsistent penalties. Evidence for FIA International Court of Appeal.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIA International Sporting Code
