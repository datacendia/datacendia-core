## SECTION A: Tax & Accounting (Scenarios 1–25)

### Scenario 1: CCH AnswerConnect AI — Tax Research Supervision
**Decision Type:** Tax
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** A Big Four tax partner uses CCH AnswerConnect AI to research a complex partnership allocation issue. The AI cites a Revenue Ruling that was modified by a subsequent Notice. The advice, based on the modified ruling without the subsequent limitation, leads the client to adopt an aggressive tax position. The IRS challenges. Accuracy penalty: $890K.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Tax research supervision: AI source citations verified — modification status confirmed for each ruling and notice. Circular 230 practitioner sign-off on each research output. Evidence for IRS audit.
**Applicable Regulations:** IRC, Circular 230, ABA Opinion 512

### Scenario 2: CCH AI — State Tax Nexus Analysis
**Decision Type:** Tax
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** CCH AI analyses economic nexus across 50 states for an e-commerce client. The AI applies pre-Wayfair thresholds for 3 states that updated their nexus standards in 2025. The client under-collects sales tax in those states. Combined assessment: $1.4M.
**Datacendia's Solution:** State tax supervision: AI nexus analysis reviewed per state, current thresholds confirmed, compliance requirements verified, SALT practitioner sign-off. Evidence for state audit.
**Applicable Regulations:** Wayfair, state economic nexus laws, Circular 230

### Scenario 3: CCH AI — International Tax Treaty
**Decision Type:** Tax
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** CCH AI researches treaty benefits for a client's cross-border royalty payments. The AI applies the US-Ireland treaty rate (0% withholding) without identifying the LOB (Limitation on Benefits) provision that disqualifies the client's Irish entity. Withholding deficiency: $2.3M.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Treaty supervision: AI treaty analysis reviewed, LOB qualification confirmed, treaty benefits verified, international tax counsel sign-off. Evidence for IRS examination.
**Applicable Regulations:** IRC §894, US-Ireland Tax Treaty, LOB provisions, Circular 230

### Scenario 4: CCH AI — Transfer Pricing Documentation
**Decision Type:** Tax
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** CCH AI assists in preparing transfer pricing documentation for a multinational client. The AI selects inappropriate comparable companies, resulting in an arm's-length range that is too wide to be defensible. The IRS challenges the transfer pricing. Adjustment: $45M.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Transfer pricing supervision: AI comparable selection reviewed, arm's-length methodology confirmed, documentation requirements verified, transfer pricing specialist sign-off. Evidence for IRS exam.
**Applicable Regulations:** IRC §482, OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, Circular 230

### Scenario 5: CCH AI — Tax Provision (ASC 740)
**Decision Type:** Tax/Accounting
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** CCH AI assists in the quarterly tax provision calculation under ASC 740. The AI misclassifies a tax position's likelihood of being sustained, overstating the tax benefit. The external auditor disagrees. The 10-Q tax provision must be restated. SOX implications follow.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Tax provision supervision: AI position classification reviewed, "more likely than not" threshold confirmed, measurement step verified, tax director sign-off. Evidence for external audit.
**Applicable Regulations:** ASC 740, SOX §302/404, PCAOB, Circular 230

### Scenario 6: TeamMate+ AI — Audit Analytics
**Decision Type:** Audit
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** TeamMate+ AI analyses journal entries for fraud indicators. The AI flags 47 entries as suspicious. Manual review reveals 44 are false positives and 3 are legitimate concerns. However, the AI MISSED 2 genuinely fraudulent entries worth $3.8M because they were structured to avoid the AI's pattern detection.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Audit AI supervision: AI detection methodology documented, false positive/negative rates tracked, manual review scope confirmed, audit partner sign-off. Evidence for PCAOB inspection.
**Applicable Regulations:** PCAOB AS 2401, AICPA fraud standards, ISA 240

### Scenario 7: Kluwer Arbitration AI — Procedural Research
**Decision Type:** Arbitration
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** Kluwer AI assists in researching arbitral procedure under the UNCITRAL Model Law. The AI misidentifies a jurisdiction's adoption status — advising that the jurisdiction adopted the 2006 amendments when it only adopted the 1985 version. The procedural application relies on a provision that doesn't exist in the applicable version.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Arbitration supervision: AI jurisdictional adoption status verified, applicable version confirmed, procedural analysis reviewed, arbitration counsel sign-off. Evidence for tribunal.
**Applicable Regulations:** UNCITRAL Model Law, national arbitration acts, IBA Guidelines

### Scenario 8: CCH AI — Estate & Gift Tax
**Decision Type:** Tax
**Wolters Kluwer's Problem:** CCH AI advises on estate tax planning. **Datacendia's Solution:** Estate tax supervision: AI exemption analysis reviewed, sunset provisions confirmed, legislative risk assessed, estate planning counsel sign-off. Evidence for estate tax return.
**Applicable Regulations:** IRC §2010, TCJA sunset provisions, Circular 230
