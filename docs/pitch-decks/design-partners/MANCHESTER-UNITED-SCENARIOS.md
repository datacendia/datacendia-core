## How Datacendia Helps Manchester United

### Scenario 1: Player Transfer Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**United's Problem:** United have spent £1B+ on transfers since 2013 with mixed results. Under INEOS's new sporting director model, every AI-assisted scouting recommendation must be defensible to NYSE investors, the Glazer board, and Premier League PSR rules.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures every transfer input (fee, wages, sell-on clause, medical, agent fee) and outcome (FFP compliance, risk assessment). Cryptographically signed by sporting director and CFO. Tamper-proof receipt for Premier League, UEFA, and NYSE SOX compliance.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR, NYSE SOX

### Scenario 2: NYSE Investor Governance (SOX Compliance)
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision` + Override Accountability
**United's Problem:** As the only major football club listed on NYSE, United must comply with Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX). AI-assisted decisions affecting material financial outcomes (transfers, commercial deals, stadium investment) require documented governance for SEC filings.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Override accountability tracks when humans override AI recommendations. Drift analysis provides longitudinal compliance trends. AI governance metrics exportable for 10-K annual reports and SEC filings.
**Applicable Regulations:** SEC, SOX, NYSE listing rules

### Scenario 3: Premier League Profitability & Sustainability Rules (PSR)
**United's Problem:** Premier League PSR limits losses to £105M over three years. United's massive wage bill and transfer spending push close to limits. AI financial modelling must be auditable.
**Datacendia's Solution:** FinancialFairPlayDecision schema tracks revenue, wages, wage-to-revenue ratio, transfer spend, amortization. Auto-flags when spending approaches PSR thresholds before deals are committed.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR

### Scenario 4: Old Trafford Redevelopment (£1B+ Project)
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`
**United's Problem:** Old Trafford requires major renovation or complete rebuild — a £1-2B decision. AI-driven feasibility studies, capacity modelling, and commercial projections must be defensible to shareholders, Manchester City Council, and financiers.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures venue decision inputs (capacity, safety assessment, accessibility, cost projections), outcomes (approval, conditions, insurance). Evidence trail for planning authority, investors, and the board.
**Applicable Regulations:** Planning law, Health & Safety, NYSE investor governance

### Scenario 5: Player Safety & Concussion Protocol
