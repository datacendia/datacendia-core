## How Datacendia Helps Dortmund

### Scenario 1: Player Development Pipeline Governance
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision` + `YouthDevelopmentDecision`
**BVB's Problem:** Dortmund's business model is developing young talent and selling at massive profit — Bellingham (€103M to Real Madrid), Sancho (€85M to Man Utd), Haaland (€60M to Man City), Dembélé (€135M to Barcelona). AI-driven scouting for the next wave, development planning, and sell-timing must be defensible to BaFin, DFL, UEFA FSR, and shareholders.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures buy-side (undervaluation, development projection, comparable analysis) and sell-side (peak timing, market conditions) decisions. CendiaPrecedent tracks pipeline success rate over time. Evidence for BaFin, DFL, and shareholders.
**Applicable Regulations:** BaFin (German securities), DFL licensing, UEFA FSR, 50+1 governance

### Scenario 2: Borsa Frankfurt / BaFin Governance
**Decision Type:** `FinancialFairPlayDecision`
**BVB's Problem:** As the only major German club listed on a stock exchange, BVB must satisfy BaFin (German securities regulator) governance. AI-assisted financial decisions (transfers, wages, commercial deals) are material events requiring disclosure and governance evidence.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Override accountability, drift analysis, AI governance metrics for annual reports and BaFin compliance. Evidence for shareholder AGMs.
**Applicable Regulations:** BaFin, German Corporate Governance Code, Borsa Frankfurt listing rules

### Scenario 3: 50+1 Fan Ownership Transparency
**BVB's Problem:** Bundesliga's 50+1 rule means fans hold majority voting rights. AI-assisted decisions must be transparent to fan representatives — unlike PE-owned clubs, BVB can't hide behind board confidentiality.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence accessible (in appropriate form) for fan governance representatives. Proves AI recommendations serve the club, not external commercial interests.
**Applicable Regulations:** DFL 50+1 regulations, German association law

### Scenario 4: Signal Iduna Park Operations (81,365)
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`
**BVB's Problem:** Largest stadium in Germany. The "Yellow Wall" (Südtribüne, 25,000 standing) is unique. Safety, capacity, and accessibility for standing sections have specific regulatory requirements.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures venue decisions including standing section safety. Evidence for Dortmund city authorities.
**Applicable Regulations:** German stadium safety regulations, NRW state law
