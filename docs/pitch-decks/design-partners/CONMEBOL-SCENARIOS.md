## How Datacendia Helps CONMEBOL

### Scenario 1: Post-Corruption Governance Reform Evidence
**Decision Type:** All decision types
**CONMEBOL's Problem:** The 2015 FBI/DOJ FIFAgate investigation resulted in multiple CONMEBOL presidents and executives imprisoned for bribery, money laundering, and racketeering. CONMEBOL reformed governance under President Domínguez (2016). Every material decision must now demonstrate integrity to US DOJ (ongoing monitoring), international sponsors, and member associations — but proving reformed governance requires more than policy documents.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every AI-assisted decision produces a Regulator's Receipt with cryptographic proof, compliance mapping, participant accountability, and dissent recording. This is the "we can prove governance is reformed" infrastructure — not just policies on paper, but verifiable evidence of every decision's integrity.
**Applicable Regulations:** US DOJ compliance monitoring, Swiss association law, Paraguayan corporate law

### Scenario 2: Copa Libertadores Commercial Governance
**Decision Type:** `BroadcastRightsDecision` + `SponsorshipDecision`
**CONMEBOL's Problem:** Copa Libertadores broadcast and sponsorship rights are worth $1B+ per cycle. The 2015 scandal centred on corruption in media rights deals (Traffic Sports, Datisa). AI-driven commercial valuations and media rights negotiations must have audit-grade evidence demonstrating arm's-length dealing and competitive bidding.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures commercial deal inputs (comparable analysis, competitive bids, valuation methodology), outcomes (selected partner, terms, anti-corruption compliance). Evidence for US DOJ, sponsors, and member associations.
**Applicable Regulations:** US DOJ, anti-corruption law, competition law in 10 countries

### Scenario 3: VAR Implementation Across 10 Jurisdictions
**Decision Type:** Custom — Officiating Decision
**CONMEBOL's Problem:** CONMEBOL implements VAR across Copa Libertadores, Copa Sudamericana, and Copa América. VAR technology must work consistently across stadiums in 10 countries with varying infrastructure quality. AI officiating decisions affecting match outcomes need evidence — especially given CONMEBOL's historical integrity issues.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every VAR decision captured: inputs, confidence, referee confirmation. Consistency monitoring across different venues and countries. Evidence for clubs appealing VAR decisions.
**Applicable Regulations:** IFAB Laws of the Game, CONMEBOL VAR Protocol

**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures betting anomaly alerts across 10 jurisdictions, investigation process, law enforcement referrals. Evidence for CONMEBOL Disciplinary Committee, national prosecutors, and Interpol.
**Applicable Regulations:** National criminal law (10 countries), Interpol cooperation, CONMEBOL integrity regulations
