**Datacendia's Solution:** Every decision produces a Regulator's Receipt with full reasoning, compliance mapping, and participant accountability. **Newcastle's Problem:** PIF's wealth is effectively unlimited, but Premier League PSR limits losses to £105M over 3 years. AI-driven financial modelling must optimise spending within PSR constraints — every pound of the budget must be AI-justified.
