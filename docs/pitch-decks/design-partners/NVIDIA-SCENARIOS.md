**Decision Type:** `ExportComplianceDecision`

A single violation can trigger: $300K+ per-violation civil penalties, denial of export privileges, and criminal prosecution under IEEPA.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI export governance: per-shipment BIS compliance verification, end-user screening against Entity List/SDN/Denied Persons, intermediary ownership analysis, EAR classification verification, constructive knowledge documentation, export license application evidence. DCII seals the complete compliance audit trail with immutable timestamps. Evidence for BIS, NVIDIA legal, and board-level compliance reporting.
**Applicable Regulations:** US EAR (15 CFR Parts 730-774), BIS Entity List, ECCN 3A090/4A090, IEEPA, Wassenaar Arrangement
