## How Datacendia Helps Tennis

### Scenario 1: Hawk-Eye Live — AI Replacing Human Judges
**Decision Type:** Custom — Officiating Decision
**Tennis's Problem:** Hawk-Eye Live has replaced human line judges at the Australian Open, US Open, and most ATP/WTA events. AI now makes every in/out call — thousands per match, affecting outcomes worth millions in prize money and rankings. Players have no mechanism to challenge Hawk-Eye accuracy beyond the visual replay. If Hawk-Eye is miscalibrated, there's no independent audit trail.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every Hawk-Eye call captured: camera inputs (12+ cameras), ball trajectory calculation, confidence score, calibration status, margin of error. Cryptographically hashed. Players/federations can request a Regulator's Receipt for any call. Drift analysis tracks Hawk-Eye accuracy across courts, conditions, and tournaments.
**Applicable Regulations:** ITF Rules of Tennis, Grand Slam regulations, ATP/WTA rulebooks

### Scenario 2: Tennis Integrity Agency (TIA) — Match-Fixing Detection
**Decision Type:** `MatchIntegrityDecision`
**Tennis's Problem:** Tennis has significant match-fixing exposure, particularly at lower-tier Challenger and ITF events where players earn little prize money. The TIA uses AI to monitor betting patterns across 70,000+ professional matches annually. AI flags suspicious matches — investigation evidence must satisfy national criminal courts.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures TIA investigation process: AI-detected betting anomalies, player communication analysis, corruptor network mapping. Court-admissible evidence for national prosecutors. CendiaPrecedent tracks investigation consistency.
**Applicable Regulations:** Tennis Anti-Corruption Programme, national criminal law, Interpol

### Scenario 3: Grand Slam Prize Money & Revenue Distribution
**Tennis's Problem:** Grand Slams distribute $350M+ in prize money. ATP/WTA distribute ranking points and prize money across 1,000+ events. AI-driven allocation models must be transparent — players and national federations challenge distribution fairness.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Decision evidence for prize money and ranking allocation. CendiaPrecedent tracks consistency.
**Applicable Regulations:** Grand Slam Board rules, ATP/WTA bylaws

### Scenario 4: Player Ranking Algorithm Governance
**Tennis's Problem:** ATP/WTA rankings determine entry into tournaments, seedings, and earnings. The ranking algorithm is AI-assisted. Players' careers depend on its fairness. Post-COVID ranking freezes and adjustments were contentious.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Ranking calculation evidence with full methodology. CendiaPrecedent tracks ranking consistency. Evidence for CAS if challenged.
**Applicable Regulations:** ATP/WTA ranking rules, CAS

### Scenario 5: Anti-Doping
**Decision Type:** `AntiDopingDecision`
**Tennis's Problem:** ITIA manages anti-doping for tennis. Jannik Sinner (world #1) tested positive for clostebol in 2024 — WADA appealed his acquittal to CAS. Chain of custody and testing evidence critical.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Complete chain-of-custody. Evidence for CAS appeals (Sinner case precedent).
**Applicable Regulations:** WADA Code, Tennis Anti-Doping Programme
