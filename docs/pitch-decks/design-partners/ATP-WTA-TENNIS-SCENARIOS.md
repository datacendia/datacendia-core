## SECTION A: AI Officiating & Technology (Scenarios 1–25)

### Scenario 1: Hawk-Eye Live — AI Replacing Human Line Judges
**Decision Type:** Custom — Officiating Decision
**Tennis's Problem:** Hawk-Eye Live has replaced human line judges at the Australian Open, US Open, and most ATP/WTA events. AI makes every in/out call — thousands per match, affecting outcomes worth millions in prize money and rankings. Players have no mechanism to challenge accuracy beyond the visual replay. No independent audit trail if miscalibrated.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every Hawk-Eye call captured: camera inputs (12+ cameras), ball trajectory calculation, confidence score, calibration status, margin of error. Cryptographically hashed. Players/federations can request a Regulator's Receipt for any call.
**Applicable Regulations:** ITF Rules of Tennis, Grand Slam regulations, ATP/WTA rulebooks

### Scenario 2: Hawk-Eye Calibration Drift Detection
**Tennis's Problem:** Hawk-Eye cameras must be calibrated before each session. Environmental conditions (heat, humidity, wind) can cause drift mid-match. A drifted system making wrong calls at 6-6 in a Grand Slam final is an existential risk.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Drift analysis tracks Hawk-Eye accuracy across courts, conditions, and tournaments. Alerts when calibration deviation exceeds thresholds.
**Applicable Regulations:** ITF ELC Protocol, Grand Slam technical regulations

### Scenario 3: Hawk-Eye Surface Performance Comparison
