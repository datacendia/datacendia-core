## Why FDA 21 CFR Part 11 + EU AI Act Is The Trigger

**21 CFR Part 11** requires electronic records and electronic signatures in FDA-regulated environments to be trustworthy, reliable, and equivalent to paper records and handwritten signatures. AI-assisted decisions using Veeva data are electronic records subject to Part 11 requirements.

**EU AI Act Annex III** classifies AI systems used in medical devices, clinical trials, drug safety monitoring, and healthcare as high-risk — requiring documented human oversight, conformity assessment, and technical documentation.

**ICH E6(R3) GCP** (2023) requires documented oversight of computerised systems used in clinical trials — including AI systems that process clinical data.

Every AI-assisted decision in Veeva's environment faces at least one of these requirements. None have systematic cryptographic documentation. Datacendia is that documentation.
