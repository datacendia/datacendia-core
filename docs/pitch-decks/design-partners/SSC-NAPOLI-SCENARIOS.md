**Decision Type:** `FFPComplianceDecision`
**Napoli's Problem:** COVISOC (Commissione di Vigilanza sulle Società di Calcio) is FIGC's financial monitoring body — scrutinising every Serie A club's financial health through quarterly and annual reporting. Napoli's sell-high/buy-smart model creates volatile financial patterns: massive transfer income in sale windows (€200M+ post-Scudetto 2023) followed by reinvestment periods. COVISOC must be satisfied that Napoli's financial position is sustainable despite these swings. De Laurentiis's Filmauro ownership means corporate group reporting adds complexity. Italian Decreto Legislativo 91/2014 (società sportive professionistiche) governs professional sports company financial requirements. Failure to satisfy COVISOC can result in points deductions, registration blocks, or exclusion from Serie A.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI financial governance: real-time COVISOC compliance monitoring, quarterly reporting preparation, transfer income volatility documentation, sell-high/buy-smart financial model evidence, Filmauro group reporting. Evidence for COVISOC, FIGC licensing, and De Laurentiis governance.
**Applicable Regulations:** FIGC COVISOC regulations, Italian D.Lgs. 91/2014, FIGC Norme Organizzative Interne della FIGC (NOIF)

### Scenario 52: UEFA FSR — Break-Even Compliance Under the Sell-High Model
**Decision Type:** `FFPMonitoringDecision`
**Napoli's Problem:** UEFA Financial Sustainability Regulations (FSR, replacing FFP) require: break-even compliance (€60M aggregate loss limit over 3 years), squad cost ratio (max 70% of revenue on player costs), and no overdue payables. Napoli's sell-high model means transfer profits are essential for break-even — a season without major sales could breach FSR. Champions League revenue (~€80-100M per campaign) is critical, but post-Scudetto underperformance (failing to qualify) would dramatically reduce revenue while the squad cost ratio remains high from replacement investment. De Laurentiis's financial discipline has historically kept Napoli profitable — AI must ensure this continues.
**Datacendia's Solution:** AI FSR governance: break-even projection modelling, squad cost ratio monitoring, transfer profit dependency analysis, Champions League qualification impact modelling, overdue payables tracking. Evidence for UEFA Club Financial Control Body (CFCB) and Filmauro financial governance.
**Applicable Regulations:** UEFA Financial Sustainability Regulations 2022, UEFA Club Licensing Regulations, FIGC licensing
