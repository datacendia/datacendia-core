## How Datacendia Helps Tottenham

### Scenario 1: $1.2B Multi-Use Stadium AI Operations
**Decision Type:** `VenueDecision`
**Spurs' Problem:** The Tottenham Hotspur Stadium ($1.2B) is the most technologically advanced in football — retractable pitch (revealing NFL-spec artificial turf beneath), 62,850 capacity, Gravity bar, Sky Walk. AI-driven operations for football matchdays, NFL London games, concerts, and events. Each use-case has different safety, capacity, and regulatory requirements.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Every venue decision captured: mode switching (football/NFL/concert), capacity configuration, safety assessments, accessibility, emergency plans. Evidence for Haringey Council, NFL partnership compliance, and ENIC governance.
**Applicable Regulations:** Safety of Sports Grounds Act, NFL venue standards, local authority licensing

### Scenario 2: NFL London Partnership Governance
**Spurs' Problem:** Tottenham host 2+ NFL regular season games per year under a long-term deal. AI-driven scheduling, commercial modelling, and cross-sport operational decisions must satisfy both the Premier League and NFL simultaneously — different regulatory frameworks, different safety standards, different commercial models.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Cross-jurisdiction conflict detection between Premier League and NFL requirements. Decision evidence for dual-sport governance.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League, NFL international game rules, UK/US regulatory overlap

### Scenario 3: Transfer Governance Under Financial Constraints
**Decision Type:** `PlayerTransferDecision`
**Spurs' Problem:** Unlike PIF/QSI-backed rivals, Tottenham operate with self-sustaining finances. AI-driven scouting must maximise value — every transfer must be justified against the stadium debt repayment schedule and Premier League PSR.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Captures transfer inputs with financial constraint compliance. Auto-flags when deals breach budget parameters. CendiaPrecedent tracks value-for-money consistency.
**Applicable Regulations:** Premier League PSR, UEFA FSR, ENIC governance

### Scenario 4: Daniel Levy Override Governance
**Spurs' Problem:** Daniel Levy is one of football's most hands-on chairmen — personally involved in transfer negotiations. When Levy overrides AI/sporting director recommendations, that override needs documentation.
**Datacendia's Solution:** Override accountability captures chairman overrides with justification and accepted risks.
