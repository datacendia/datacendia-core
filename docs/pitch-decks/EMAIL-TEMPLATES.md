## TIPS FOR SENDING

1. **Always personalize** — Replace every placeholder. Generic emails get ignored.
2. **Attach the PDF** (not the PPTX) — PDFs are universally viewable and look professional.
3. **Subject line matters** — Keep it specific. Include the firm name for investor emails.
4. **Send Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am** in the recipient's timezone.
5. **Follow up once at Day 5**, once at Day 12. After that, move on.
6. **Never BCC multiple investors** on the same email.
7. **Track opens** if possible (Superhuman, Mixmax, etc.) to time follow-ups.
8. **Data room ready** — Have your data room link ready before sending. If they respond, you need to move fast.
