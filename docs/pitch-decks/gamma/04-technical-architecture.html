DCII

Cryptographic Audit Infrastructure

Truth

Verification layer. Every claim made by an agent is cross-referenced against data sources. Confidence scores based on evidence strength.

Notary

Timestamping service. SHA-256 hash of every deliberation step with RFC 3161 timestamp. Immutable, tamper-evident chain.

Witness

Attestation protocol. Independent verification that deliberation followed the declared mode and all agents participated.

Timestamp

Precise temporal ordering of all events. Enables "decision replay" — reconstructing exact deliberation sequence.

Similarity

Deduplication and pattern matching. Identifies when a current decision resembles past deliberations, surfacing historical context.

Memory

Institutional knowledge persistence. Agents learn from past deliberations without retraining — organizational wisdom accumulates.

For auditors & regulators: Hand them the DCII hash chain — every agent's reasoning, every vote, every dissent, cryptographically timestamped and tamper-evident. This is the audit infrastructure that AI governance demands.
