How It Works

The Council™ — 3-Step Deliberation

01

Pose a Decision

"Should we approve this $50M loan?" or "Which treatment pathway?" or "Is this contract EU AI Act compliant?"

02

50+ agents Deliberate

CFO analyzes financials. CISO checks security. Risk identifies threats. Legal checks compliance. Red Team challenges. Each votes with confidence.

03

Decision Package

Recommendation + confidence score + dissenting views + evidence trail + suggested actions. Every step hashed and timestamped.

The difference: ChatGPT gives one answer. Datacendia gives a boardroom debate — with dissents preserved, not silenced, and a cryptographic proof chain for every step.
