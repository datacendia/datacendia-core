## Post 2 — The Evidence Package
**Post following Monday**

The insight that changed how I designed Datacendia:

A regulator doesn't need to log into your system. They need a file they can open on an air-gapped laptop — no internet, no trust in you.

So that's what we built.

Every AI decision produces a self-contained HTML file: reasoning chain, dissenting opinions, dual cryptographic signatures, a Merkle proof, and a standalone JavaScript verifier.

No Datacendia account required to verify it. No server call. No trust in us.

That's not a UX decision. It's an architectural one.

Compliance without dependency is what "sovereign" actually means.
