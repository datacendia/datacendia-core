## Tagline
Forensic-grade AI audit trails — prove your AI decisions to any regulator, offline

## Description (250 words max)
The EU AI Act enforcement date is August 2, 2026. Article 14 requires human oversight audit trails for High-Risk AI. Article 12 requires logging.

Most enterprises are using dashboards or spreadsheets to track this. Dashboards are not evidence. A regulator doesn't log into your system — they need a file they can open on an air-gapped laptop.

Datacendia produces a Regulator's Receipt for every AI decision: a self-contained HTML file with the full reasoning chain, dissenting opinions, dual cryptographic signatures, a Merkle proof, and a standalone JavaScript verifier. No account required to verify it. No server call. No trust in us.

Works offline. Runs on your infrastructure. Your data never leaves.

Built for financial services, healthcare, and government contractors deploying High-Risk AI under EU AI Act, Basel III, HIPAA, and DOD AI ethics requirements.

Open source core. Self-hosted. Air-gapped deployment available.
