# Management Assertion
## Regarding the Accompanying Description of Datacendia's System
## and the Suitability of the Design of Controls

**Datacendia, LLC**
**As of Date:** April 15, 2026

---

We are responsible for:

1. **Preparing the accompanying description** of Datacendia's AI Governance and Decision Intelligence platform ("the System") based on the criteria for a description of a service organisation's system in DC sections 1 through 9 of the AICPA Guide *Reporting on an Examination of a Service Organization's Controls Over a System Relevant to Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, or Privacy* ("the description criteria"). The description is intended to provide users with information about the system, particularly the controls designed to meet the criteria for the security and availability categories of the trust services criteria, throughout the period from **[engagement start date]** to the as-of date of **April 15, 2026**.

2. **Providing an assertion** about whether, based on our knowledge:
   - The accompanying description fairly presents the Datacendia system that was designed and implemented throughout the period from [engagement start date] to April 15, 2026, based on the description criteria.
   - The controls stated in the accompanying description were suitably designed to provide reasonable assurance that the applicable trust services criteria would be met if the controls operated effectively throughout the period from [engagement start date] to April 15, 2026.

---

## Our Assertions

We assert that:

**A. Fair Presentation of the Description**

The accompanying description of Datacendia's system as of April 15, 2026 is fairly presented in all material respects based on the description criteria.

**B. Suitability of Design of Controls**

The controls stated in the description were suitably designed to provide reasonable assurance that the applicable trust services criteria in the security and availability categories would be met if the controls operated effectively as of April 15, 2026.

---

## Inherent Limitations

The description is prepared to meet the common needs of a broad range of users and may not, therefore, include every aspect of the system that each individual user may consider important to their particular needs. Because of their nature, controls may not prevent or detect and correct all errors or omissions in processing or reporting. The projection of any evaluation of the fairness of the presentation of the description or the assessment of the suitability of the design of the controls to future periods is subject to the risk that controls may become inadequate because of changes in conditions or that the degree of compliance with the controls may deteriorate.
