# Statement of Applicability (SoA)
**ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Clause 6.1.3(d) / Annex A**
**Document Owner:** Engineering Lead / Acting CISO
**Version:** 1.0 | April 2026
**Review Cadence:** Annually

---

## Overview

This Statement of Applicability lists all 93 controls from ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, states whether each is **Included** or **Excluded**, gives the **justification**, describes **implementation evidence**, and assigns a **status**.

**Status Key:**
- ✅ **Implemented** — Control is fully in place
- 🟡 **Partial** — Control is partially implemented; gap noted
- 📋 **Planned** — Not yet implemented; target date set
- ❌ **Excluded** — Control excluded with documented justification
