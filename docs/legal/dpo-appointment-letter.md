## Scope of Responsibilities

The DPO is appointed to perform the following functions under GDPR Article 39:

**1. Inform and advise**
Inform and advise Datacendia and its employees about their obligations pursuant to applicable privacy laws.

**2. Monitor compliance**
Monitor compliance with applicable data protection laws and with Datacendia's internal privacy policies, including assignment of responsibilities, awareness-raising, training, and related audits.

**3. Provide advice on DPIAs**
Provide advice on and monitor the performance of Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) where required under GDPR Article 35.

**4. Cooperate with supervisory authorities**
Act as the primary point of contact for supervisory authorities (ICO, CNIL, DPA, ANPD, OPC, etc.) and cooperate with them on all data protection matters.

**5. Act as contact point for data subjects**
Serve as the contact point for data subjects exercising their rights under applicable privacy laws (access, erasure, restriction, portability, rectification, opt-out).

**6. Maintain ROPA**
Maintain and update the Record of Processing Activities (ROPA) under GDPR Article 30.

## Authorities and Protections

Consistent with GDPR Article 38:

- The DPO **shall not receive instructions** regarding the exercise of their tasks and shall report directly to the highest management level of Datacendia
- The DPO **shall not be dismissed or penalised** for performing their tasks
- The DPO **may fulfil other tasks and duties** provided they do not result in a conflict of interest
- The DPO **shall be provided with resources** necessary to carry out these tasks
