## Video Production Guidelines

### Technical Requirements
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264)
- **Audio**: Professional voiceover with background music
- **Captions**: Available for accessibility

### Brand Guidelines
- **Colors**: Datacendia brand palette
- **Fonts**: Inter for UI text, professional font for overlays
- **Logo**: Datacendia logo with proper spacing
- **Transitions**: Smooth, professional animations
- **Music**: Corporate, upbeat background music

### Recording Best Practices
- **Screen Recording**: High-quality screen capture with smooth cursor movement
- **Voice Recording**: Professional microphone, clear enunciation
- **Pacing**: Appropriate speed for technical content
- **Visual Focus**: Zoom in on important UI elements
- **Annotations**: Use callouts and highlights for key features

### Post-Production
- **Editing**: Professional transitions and timing
- **Graphics**: Consistent text overlays and animations
- **Color Correction**: Professional color grading
- **Audio Mixing**: Balanced voice and music levels
- **Export**: Optimized for web and social media platforms

### Distribution Strategy
- **Website**: Embed on product pages and homepage
- **YouTube**: Optimize for search with proper titles and descriptions
- **LinkedIn**: B2B focused content with professional messaging
- **Twitter**: Short clips with key features highlighted
- **Email Marketing**: Include in newsletters and sales sequences

### Performance Tracking
- **View Counts**: Monitor engagement across platforms
