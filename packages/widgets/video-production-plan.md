# Video Production Plan for Datacendia Demo

## Production Schedule

### Week 1: Pre-Production
- **Day 1-2**: Script finalization and storyboarding
- **Day 3**: Equipment setup and testing
- **Day 4**: Screen recording preparation
- **Day 5**: Initial recording sessions

### Week 2: Production & Post-Production
- **Day 1-2**: Main recording sessions
- **Day 3**: B-roll and additional footage
- **Day 4**: Editing and post-production
- **Day 5**: Final review and optimization

## Equipment Requirements

### Hardware
- **Camera**: 4K webcam or DSLR (Sony A6400 or equivalent)
- **Microphone**: Professional USB mic (Blue Yeti or Rode NT-USB)
- **Lighting**: Ring light or softbox lighting setup
- **Computer**: High-performance machine for smooth screen recording

### Software
- **Screen Recording**: Camtasia, OBS Studio, or ScreenFlow
- **Video Editing**: Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or DaVinci Resolve
- **Audio Editing**: Adobe Audition or Audacity
- **Graphics**: Adobe After Effects or Canva for overlays

## Recording Setup

### Environment
- **Lighting**: Soft, even lighting avoiding harsh shadows
- **Background**: Professional office setting or branded background
- **Sound**: Quiet environment with minimal echo
- **Camera Position**: Eye-level, stable tripod

### Screen Recording Settings
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps
- **Bitrate**: 8-12 Mbps
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264)

### Audio Settings
- **Sample Rate**: 48kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 24-bit
- **Format**: WAV or high-quality MP3
- **Levels**: Peaking at -6dB to -3dB

## Content Production

### 2-Minute Overview Video
**Structure:**
- **0:00-0:15**: Logo animation and introduction
- **0:15-1:30**: Feature highlights with quick cuts
- **1:30-1:45**: Key benefits and value proposition
- **1:45-2:00**: Call to action and contact information

**Recording Plan:**
- Screen recording of demo interface
- Professional voiceover narration
- Animated text overlays for key points
- Background music (corporate, upbeat)

### 5-Minute Detailed Demo
**Structure:**
- **0:00-0:30**: Introduction and context setting
- **0:30-2:00**: PII Scanner deep dive
- **2:00-3:30**: Evidence Viewer demonstration
- **3:30-4:30**: Council Status Badges overview
- **4:30-5:00**: Conclusion and next steps

**Recording Plan:**
- Presenter on camera (professional appearance)
- Screen recording with cursor movements
- Zoom-ins on important UI elements
- Callouts and highlights for key features

### 30-Second Social Clips
**Structure per clip:**
- **0:00-0:05**: Hook/Problem statement
- **0:05-0:20**: Solution demonstration
- **0:20-0:30**: Call to action

**Clip Topics:**
- PII detection capabilities
- Cryptographic proof features
- Interactive elements
- Industry solutions

## Post-Production

### Editing Workflow
1. **Import and Organize**: Raw footage and audio files
2. **Sync Audio**: Align voiceover with screen recording
3. **Cut Assembly**: Build rough edit following script
4. **Graphics**: Add text overlays, callouts, and branding
5. **Audio Mix**: Balance voice, music, and sound effects
6. **Color Correction**: Professional color grading
7. **Export**: Multiple formats for different platforms

### Graphics Requirements
- **Lower Thirds**: Presenter name and title
- **Text Overlays**: Key talking points and statistics
- **Callouts**: Highlight important UI elements
- **Transitions**: Smooth, professional animations
- **Branding**: Consistent Datacendia branding

### Audio Processing
- **Noise Reduction**: Remove background noise
- **Equalization**: Enhance voice clarity
- **Compression**: Even out volume levels
- **Music**: Add appropriate background music
- **Mixing**: Balance all audio elements
